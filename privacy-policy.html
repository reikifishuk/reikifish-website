PRIVACY AND DATA PROTECTION

Your information should be treated with care.

This policy explains what personal information ReikiFish may collect, why it may be used, how it is protected and the choices and rights available to you.

Policy summary

  • There are no website accounts or user logins.
  • Support Finder preview prompts are not stored.
  • Contact enquiries are used only to respond.
  • Optional analytics require prior consent.
  • You may exercise your UK data rights.

01

Who we are

ReikiFish is operated by Andy Fish trading as ReikiFish. For the purposes of UK data protection law, Andy Fish trading as ReikiFish is the data controller for personal information processed through this website.

Privacy enquiries may be sent to [email protected] .

ReikiFish is committed to handling personal information in accordance with the UK General Data Protection Regulation, the Data Protection Act 2018 and, where relevant, the Privacy and Electronic Communications Regulations.

02

Scope of this policy

This policy applies to personal information processed through reikifish.com, including the contact form, Support Finder, Knowledge Hub, coaching information pages and related website services.

It does not govern independent websites reached through external links. Those organisations are responsible for their own privacy practices.

03

Information we may process

Information you provide

When you contact ReikiFish, the information provided may include:

  • Your name
  • Your email address
  • Your telephone number, where supplied
  • The content of your message or enquiry
  • Information you choose to provide in correspondence

Technical information

The website hosting and security infrastructure may automatically process limited technical information, such as:

  • Internet Protocol address
  • Browser and device type
  • Requested pages and access times
  • Referring website or page
  • Security and error information

This information may be required to deliver, protect and diagnose the website.

Please minimise sensitive information. Do not send case numbers, passwords, complete medical records, identity documents or information about another person unless it is genuinely necessary and lawful to do so.

04

Support Finder and temporary prompts

The current Support Finder preview works within your browser. Prompt text and temporary chat history are not sent to ReikiFish, saved to an account or retained after the page is closed or refreshed.

There are currently no user accounts, registrations or login profiles associated with the Support Finder.

The interface may temporarily hold prompt text, category choices and location information in the memory of the open browser page so it can display the current search. That temporary information is cleared when the page is refreshed or closed.

Before future functionality begins sending searches to a server, directory provider or artificial intelligence service, this policy and the notice beside the prompt will be updated to explain:

  • Exactly what information is transmitted
  • Which provider receives it
  • The lawful basis for processing
  • How long it is retained
  • Whether information leaves the United Kingdom
  • How users can exercise their rights

The Support Finder provides information and signposting. It does not make legal or similarly significant automated decisions about individuals.

06

Google Analytics and optional cookies

Google Analytics is planned but is not currently active on the website. It will not be enabled until appropriate cookie controls are installed.

If Google Analytics is introduced, it may process information about page visits, interactions, device type, approximate location and traffic sources. Optional analytics cookies will be blocked unless the visitor actively consents.

Visitors will be able to reject optional cookies as easily as they can accept them and will be able to change their preference later.

Before activation, ReikiFish will document the exact Analytics configuration, retention period, cookies, Google processing arrangements and any applicable international-transfer safeguards.

PAYMENTS

Bookings, payments and confirmations

When you request or purchase a coaching session, ReikiFish may process your name, email address, telephone number, selected service, appointment date and time, booking reference, payment status and information you choose to include with the booking. This information is used to arrange and administer the requested service, prevent duplicate bookings, provide confirmations and deal with cancellations, refunds, disputes or support enquiries.

Payments are processed securely by PayPal. ReikiFish does not receive or store your complete card number, bank-account credentials or PayPal password. PayPal processes payment and fraud-prevention information under its own privacy terms. You can read the PayPal Privacy Statement before completing checkout.

Booking records are stored using Cloudflare infrastructure. Mailgun may process the name, email address and booking details needed to deliver confirmation and administrative messages. These providers act under their respective data-protection arrangements and may process information outside the United Kingdom using applicable safeguards.

The usual lawful basis for processing booking information is taking requested steps before entering a contract and performing the coaching-service contract. Legitimate interests may also apply to service security, fraud prevention, record keeping and resolving disputes. Legal obligation applies where records must be retained for tax, accounting or regulatory purposes.

Booking and transaction records are retained only for as long as reasonably necessary to provide the service and meet accounting, tax, chargeback, dispute-resolution and other legal requirements. Information that is no longer required will be securely deleted or anonymised. You may exercise the data-protection rights described later in this policy.

07

Sharing and service providers

Personal information is not sold. It may be processed by trusted providers where necessary to operate the website and respond to enquiries.

Relevant providers may include:

  • Mailgun, which supports delivery of contact-form emails
  • Website hosting, security and infrastructure providers
  • Professional advisers where reasonably necessary
  • Google Analytics only if enabled after consent

Information may also be disclosed where required by law, a valid court order, safeguarding obligations or the prevention and investigation of crime.

08

International transfers

Some technology providers may process information outside the United Kingdom. Where UK personal information is transferred internationally, ReikiFish will use an applicable lawful transfer mechanism and appropriate safeguards.

These may include UK adequacy regulations, the UK International Data Transfer Agreement, the UK Addendum to approved contractual clauses or another lawful safeguard.

09

How long information is retained

General contact enquiries are normally retained for no longer than 12 months after the last meaningful communication, unless a longer period is reasonably required for an ongoing service, dispute, safeguarding matter or legal obligation.

Technical security logs are retained according to the operational and security schedules of the relevant hosting provider.

Support Finder preview prompts are not retained by ReikiFish. Planned Google Analytics retention will be configured and disclosed before Analytics is enabled.

Information that is no longer needed will be deleted, anonymised or securely disposed of.

10

Security

Reasonable technical and organisational safeguards are used to protect personal information against unauthorised access, alteration, loss, disclosure or destruction.

No internet service can promise absolute security. Visitors should avoid sending unnecessary sensitive information through ordinary website forms or email.

11

Your data protection rights

Depending on the circumstances and lawful basis, UK data protection law may provide rights to:

  • Request access to your personal information
  • Request correction of inaccurate information
  • Request deletion of information
  • Request restriction of processing
  • Object to processing
  • Receive portable information where applicable
  • Withdraw consent at any time
  • Challenge qualifying solely automated decisions

Requests may be sent to [email protected] . Identity verification may be requested where reasonably necessary to protect personal information.

There is normally no charge for exercising these rights. A response will normally be provided within one month, subject to lawful extensions or exceptions.

12

Children and young people

The website does not provide accounts for children and does not knowingly seek to collect personal information directly from children through the Support Finder.

A child or young person needing urgent help should contact a trusted adult, appropriate safeguarding service or emergency service. They should not enter identifying or highly sensitive information into the Support Finder preview.
