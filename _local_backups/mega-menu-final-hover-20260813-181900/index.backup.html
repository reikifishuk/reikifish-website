FEATURED BOOK

Walking the Grey

BY REIKI FISH

A journey through stillness, truth and endurance — and the difficult ground between darkness and light.

Walking the Grey explores alienation, abuse, emotional survival and reclamation through the perspective of a father learning to remain steady when certainty, identity and family life are tested.

It is a book about what happens after the noise: when reaction gives way to reflection, pain becomes understanding, and strength no longer needs to announce itself.

PSYCHOLOGY

Understanding the Mind

The psychology explored throughout Walking the Grey is grounded in patterns of defence, attachment and recovery rather than spectacle.

DARVO

The defensive pattern of denial, blame and reversal that can distort accountability and intimacy.

Attachment

How early experiences shape safety, trust and the way we reach for connection.

Emotional Regulation

Returning to steadiness without denying the depth of what has been felt or endured.

Nervous System

The body as a witness to stress, survival and the quiet work of recovery.

Dark Tetrad

A careful study of the shadow forms that can corrupt power, intimacy and moral clarity.

Trauma Recovery

Recovery as a slow, structured return to self rather than a sudden breakthrough.
