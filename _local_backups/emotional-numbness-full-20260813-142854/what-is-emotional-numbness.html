This short foundation page establishes the subject carefully without treating one experience as a diagnosis. A complete evidence led article will be added through the established two block content process.

Understanding

Why context matters

The same outward experience can have different causes, meanings and consequences. Useful understanding considers duration, intensity, circumstances, physical health, safety, relationships and the person's own account.

Support

Using this information responsibly

This page provides education rather than individual assessment. Persistent, severe or unexplained changes should be discussed with an appropriately qualified professional. Seek urgent local help when somebody cannot remain safe or may be experiencing a medical emergency.
