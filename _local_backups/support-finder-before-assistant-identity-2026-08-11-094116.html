IMPORTANT SAFETY INFORMATION

If someone is in immediate danger, use emergency services.

The Support Finder is an information and signposting tool. It is not an emergency service and does not replace legal, medical, safeguarding or mental health advice.

In the United Kingdom, call 999 when there is immediate danger. If you are outside the UK, use the emergency number for your location.
