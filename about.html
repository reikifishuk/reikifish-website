Who I am

Experience shaped by real responsibility.

For more than 25 years I have worked across management, team leadership, performance, sales, marketing, project delivery and technology. I understand the pressure of making decisions, guiding people and remaining composed when circumstances are demanding.

My development includes nationally recognised qualifications and more than 37 professional certificates covering areas such as management practice, project and team management, leadership, sales psychology, marketing, technology, trauma-informed coaching, foundational psychology and mental-health-related learning.

Qualifications matter, but they are only part of responsible practice. I continue to study, review evidence and recognise the limits of my professional role.

Why this work is personal

I understand ambiguous loss from the inside.

I know what it is to grieve a relationship with a child who is still alive. This form of ambiguous loss can be especially difficult because there may be no clear ending, no shared ritual and no simple path through the grief.

I have lived through prolonged adversity, family separation and circumstances in which I felt unheard while navigating difficult family and legal processes. I do not use my public work to identify, accuse or diagnose anyone involved.

“The experience taught me that regulation is not surrender. It is how we regain enough clarity to decide what comes next.”

The Grey

The Grey helped save me.

The Grey became my practical framework for pausing, regulating and responding from a steadier position rather than being driven entirely by fear, anger or emotional overwhelm.

It did not erase what happened, replace professional healthcare or make grief disappear. It helped me stop losing myself inside circumstances I could not fully control.

That experience now informs how I help others create breathing room, organise difficult thoughts, protect their energy and make more deliberate choices.

Professional boundaries

Clear about what coaching is and is not.

I am not a psychologist, psychiatrist, psychotherapist, counsellor, medical professional or emergency intervention service. I do not diagnose mental-health conditions or personality disorders.

I do not assess absent third parties, label people as narcissists or provide clinical or legal opinions. Coaching does not replace medical, psychological, legal, safeguarding or crisis support.

If you are in immediate danger or experiencing a mental-health emergency, call 999 or attend A&E. In England you can also contact NHS 111. Samaritans can be reached free at 116 123.

A steadier next step

You do not have to carry everything alone.

I cannot promise a legal outcome, restore a relationship or remove grief. I can offer calm, structured and non-judgemental support while you regulate, think more clearly and decide how you want to move forward.
